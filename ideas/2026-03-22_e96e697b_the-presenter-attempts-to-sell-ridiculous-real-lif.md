## Outline

- 1. **The Pitch**: The presenter introduces the business, highlighting its most outrageous features and trying to sell it as a legitimate opportunity.
- 2. **The Exposé**: As the presenter clicks through the PowerPoint presentation, they reveal the flaws and absurdities of the business, often with hilarious results (e.g., a cryptocurrency that's actually just a shell company).
- 3. **The Testimonials**: The presenter presents fake testimonials from "satisfied customers" who have made fortunes using the presented business.
- 4. **The Twist**: Just when the viewer thinks they've seen it all, the presenter reveals the ultimate twist: this business is real (or at least has been tried by someone, somewhere).
