# Water erosion

How fast water wears a surface or a bed away, compared across materials.

* **Mechanisms:** sand-laden water hitting a surface (Finnie), flowing water on a bed or bank (Shields threshold, excess shear) and sliding wear (Archard).
* **How sure:** the erosion constants are order-of-magnitude starter values. Put erosion test results (speed and erosion ratio) in a table and Calibrate fits K and n (`sample_data/erosion_tests.csv`).

The sample data is made up to show the format. Replace the link with your own folder, a Ledger or a Connections graph in the Model view.

Run the tests with **Test** in the Build tab. Open the **Model** view to set the inputs and see the design sheet.
