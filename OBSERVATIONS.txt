[O1] 2026-06-30 — "how many files are in the vault" feels slow      STATUS: DONE
  Observed:  Asking the count routes to the analyst's file-count, shows the
             total number of files it can see, then "computes from data" and
             then "reviews the matching files". The whole thing feels slow for
             what is a trivial question.
  Notes (unconfirmed): the deterministic file-count direct-route returns the
             answer essentially instantly (a cheap os.walk census, no model).
             So the slowness is almost certainly the layers AROUND it, not the
             count:
               1) the council still runs full model deliberation + Writer
                  synthesis on top of the instant answer (model time, slow on
                  CPU / a weak model), and
               2) vault search / "[VAULT MATCH]" file-matching + injection may
                  still run for the same query ("reviews the matching files").
             Likely fixes to weigh later: for a pure file-count intent, skip the
             vault-match pass and/or short-circuit to a terse direct reply
             without a full multi-role deliberation. Confirm by watching the
             [MODEL_CALL] / [DEBUG inject] console lines for where the time goes.
  Resolution (2026-06-30): added a FAST-ANSWER SHORT-CIRCUIT. When the analyst
             direct-routes (file count, data/stats summary, fresh derived
             result, or a saved collection), the answer is rendered instantly
             with NO model call and NO multi-role deliberation; an "⤢ Expand
             with council" button re-runs the full deliberation on demand.
             Toggle in ⚙ Engine → "Fast direct answers" (default on).
